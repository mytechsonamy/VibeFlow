# VibeFlow v2.18.0 — Loop-Status Dashboard (capstone)

**Sprint 30** (backlog B4) — and the capstone of the autonomous-loop arc.
Sprints 20–29 built a loop that mines consensus history, tunes config,
auto-applies and auto-reverts, self-demotes, shares a privacy-safe signal
across projects, and walks whole phases unattended. Its *state*, though,
lived in half a dozen files; `/vibeflow:status` showed only a slice.
v2.18.0 gives the loop its own pane of glass.

## `/vibeflow:loop-status`

A dedicated, **read-only** dashboard that consolidates every loop surface
into one human view **and** writes a durable
`.vibeflow/reports/loop-status.md` audit:

```
VibeFlow Autonomous Loop — <project> @ <phase>

  Phase-runner:   running · step consensus-round-1 · attempt 2/3 · 1/2 steps
  Auto-apply:     applied 3 · held 1 · reverted 2
    per key:      consensus.maxIterations  3/1/2  revertRate=0.67  cooled-down  ⚠ candidate for removal
  Cooled-down:    consensus.maxIterations
  Armed watch:    consensus.maxIterations @ ARCHITECTURE
  Learning:       4 findings → /vibeflow:learning-loop-engine --mode consensus-history
  Cross-project:  consensus.maxIterations held in 3 projects  holdRate=0.67  (recommendation only)
  Recent:         CONFIG-TUNE — consensus.maxIterations 5 → 7
```

Where `/vibeflow:status` gives a quick inline glance, `/vibeflow:loop-status`
is the full pane of glass — with a durable, shareable report. It flags
keys at `revertRate ≥ 0.5` as removal candidates, surfaces the
cross-project signal as a **recommendation only** (never auto-allowlists),
and collapses to a single idle line on a project that has never looped.

## Under the hood

`hooks/scripts/loop-audit.sh` (the Sprint-25 reader behind `/vibeflow:status`)
gains two sections — `phaseRunner` (from the Sprint-21 progress ledger)
and `crossProject` (from the Sprint-28 global store, only when
`globalLearning.enabled`). Both are `null` when absent, so
`/vibeflow:status`' Autonomous Loop section is byte-for-byte unchanged —
the new fields feed only the dedicated dashboard.

Read-only throughout: the only write is the `.vibeflow/reports/loop-status.md`
report. Nothing touches `vibeflow.config.json`, `autoApply.keys`, or any
source/artifact.

See `docs/LOOP-STATUS.md` (new) and the refreshed `docs/LOOP-AUDIT.md`.
